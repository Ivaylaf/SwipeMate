# SwipeMate - User Guide

## Welcome to SwipeMate! 🎉

SwipeMate is a collaborative decision-making app that helps groups of friends decide on movies, restaurants, recipes, and board games through an intuitive swipe-based interface.

## Getting Started

### 1. Authentication
- **Login**: Use any username and password to log in (demo app)
- **Register**: Create a new account with username, email, and password
- The app will automatically log you in after registration

### 2. Home Screen
After logging in, you'll see four main categories:
- 🎬 **Movies & TV** - Find the perfect movie or series to watch
- 🍽️ **Restaurants** - Discover great dining spots in Bulgaria
- 👨‍🍳 **Recipes** - Get cooking ideas for your next meal
- 🎲 **Board Games** - Choose the perfect game for game night

### 3. Quick Actions
From the home screen, you can also:
- **Friends** - Manage your friends and friend requests
- **Match History** - View past group decisions
- **My Profile** - Check your stats and badges

## How to Use

### Creating a Match Session

1. **Select a Category** - Tap on Movies, Restaurants, Recipes, or Board Games
2. **Choose Friends** - Select which friends you want to match with (or go solo!)
3. **Set Filters** - Customize your preferences:
   - **Movies**: Genre, rating, release year, duration
   - **Restaurants**: Cuisine, district, rating
   - **Recipes**: Difficulty, cuisine, meal type, budget
   - **Board Games**: Game type, number of players, rating
4. **Start Swiping** - Begin reviewing options!

### Swiping

- **Swipe Right** or tap ❤️ to **LIKE** an option
- **Swipe Left** or tap ✕ to **REJECT** an option
- Swipe cards with your finger for a fun, interactive experience
- The app tracks progress: see how many items you've reviewed

### Getting a Match

When you like an item, the app checks if it's a match! In a real-world scenario with connected friends, the app would:
- Track everyone's swipes in real-time
- Notify the group when someone likes an item
- Show a celebration screen when everyone agrees
- Save the match to your history

*Note: In this demo, matches happen automatically when you like an item to simulate the experience.*

## Friends System

### Adding Friends
1. Go to the **Friends** screen
2. Enter a username in the search box
3. Tap "Add" to send a friend request

### Managing Friend Requests
1. Go to the **Friends** screen
2. Switch to the "Requests" tab
3. **Accept** ✓ or **Reject** ✕ requests

### Friend Status
- 🟢 **Online** - Friend is currently active
- ⚫ **Offline** - Friend is not active

## Tips & Tricks

1. **Solo Mode**: You can create sessions without selecting friends to browse content yourself
2. **Multiple Filters**: Combine multiple filters for highly specific results
3. **Quick Decisions**: If everyone's online, matches happen faster!
4. **History Review**: Use match history to remember great choices
5. **Badge Hunting**: Aim for all achievements to unlock your "power user" status
